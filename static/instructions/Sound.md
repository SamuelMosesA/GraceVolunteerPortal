# **Grace Church Sound Setup Guide**

This guide details the standard operating procedures for retrieving equipment, setting up the sound system, and managing the mix (including the new stage monitor routing). Reference: "Grace Church Sound Set Up.docx".

**General Rule:** Everything must be connected with sound *and* power\!

## **1\. Equipment Retrieval**

*Things to get out of the closet:*

* Sound board (goes in the back on the table)  
* Sound labeled plastic bin (goes in the back on the table)  
* Black stage box  
* Power labeled plastic bin  
* Audio labeled plastic bin  
* Mic labeled plastic bin  
* Black bag with speaker stands  
* Two speakers  
* One monitor  
* One to two guitar stands (depending on who is playing)  
* Two mic stands  
* One to two music stands

## **2\. Physical Setup and Connections**

### **Step 1: Stage Box**

* Place the black sound box at the back center of the stage.  
* Open both sides and plug the box into power (you may sometimes need the extension cord).  
* Take the long blue cord and thread it over the door (the one that holds the chairs on the left side of the stage as you look at it). Hook the wire on the hooks and take it all the way to the back where the sound table is.  
* Plug the blue wire into the sound board on the back right side labeled **A**.  
* **Do NOT unplug the blue wire from the black sound box please\!**

### **Step 2: Speakers**

* Take the speaker stands out of the bag and set one up on each side of the stage.  
* Make sure the knobs are tight so the speakers will not fall. Adjust them equally (hole two or three is fine).  
* Put the speaker in the front hole (it will feel like it is not in well and tilt forward).  
* Plug speakers into power. The power cable should go into the grey **Input A**.  
* Find the blue-ended audio cords and plug them into the blue hole on the speakers.  
* Plug the opposite end of those audio cords into the black stage box in **outputs 7 and 8** (located under the cajon labeled slots).

### **Step 3: Stage Monitor (Physical Connection)**

* Plug the floor monitor into power.  
* Plug an audio cord into the MIC input slot on the monitor.  
* **New Routing Method:** Connect the other end of the cord to **output port 4** on the stage box. *(Note: The previous routing method used port 3, but port 4 is the recommended new standard for Bus 8 routing. The old way still works, but this is preferred).*

### **Step 4: Mics & Instruments**

* Set up 1-3 mic stands depending on who is singing.  
* Add microphones to the stands.  
* Connect an audio cord to Mic 1 and connect it to the **Lead Vocal** slot on the stage box.  
* Connect an audio cord to Mic 2 and connect it to the **Vocal 1** slot on the stage box.  
* Connect guitar audio lines into **Guitar 1** for the lead, and **Bass** for the second guitar/bass.  
* **Note\!\!** Please do NOT unplug the Preach and Translate lines on the top right of the stage box\! These stay plugged in as they are for the cordless mics.  
* Lay the rug down over the loose lines on the stage to prevent tripping.

## **3\. Stage Monitor Routing & Mixing (New Method)**

The stage monitor is now routed using a dedicated bus (**Bus 8**) for better mix control.

**To control this new routing:**

1. **Accessing the Bus:** On the mixing console, press the **"BUS 1-8"** button located in the middle section. This switches the faders on the right side to control the busses.  
2. **Master Monitor Control:** The furthest right fader is now **Bus 8**. This fader controls the overall volume of the stage monitor mix. You can use the mute button above this fader to mute the monitor entirely.  
   * **Important Note:** The routing sound on the monitor is a fader on top of the main output. Therefore, if the main output is muted or its volume is low, the monitor will also be low or muted. *(Bus 8 was selected as it is the closest to the main mix fader).*  
3. **Adjusting Individual Channels (Sends on Fader):**  
   * Select **Bus 8** by pressing its "Select" button on the right side.  
   * Press the **"SENDS ON FADER"** button (located under "BUSSES/GROUPS" in the middle of the panel). This button will start blinking.  
   * *Notice:* The faders on the left side will change positions. They now control how much volume each individual channel sends to Bus 8 (the stage monitor mix), *not* the main house mix.  
   * Adjust individual channel levels as needed for the monitor (see Best Practices below).  
4. **Return to Main Mix:** **Crucial Step:** Once you are finished adjusting the monitor mix, do not forget to press the **"SENDS ON FADER"** button again. It should stop blinking, and the left faders will return to controlling the main house mix.

## **4\. Stage Monitor Mix Logic & Best Practices**

To optimize sound quality and prevent feedback, follow these guidelines for the current stage monitor logic:

* **Preachers' Mics:** Keep these **muted** in the monitor mix. There is no need for preachers to hear themselves through the stage monitor, and this avoids feedback.  
* **Singers' Mics:** Keep these levels **significantly lower**. Vocal mics are normally a source of feedback loops through the stage monitor.  
* **Direct-In Instruments (Guitars, Bass, Keyboard):** These can and should be the **loudest** elements in the stage mic. Because they are connected directly to the stage box (not via microphones placed in front of them), they do not cause feedback and are essential for leading singers to clearly hear the music.

## **5\. Alternative Setups**

**Cajon / Drum Set**

* *Current Practice:* Over the last few Sundays, we are **not using microphones** for the cajon or drum set. In our hall, they are already loud enough, so unless we start to record the singing, we can skip putting mics in front of them.  
* *If miking is required:* Get the cajon out (brown box on the shelf in the back corner). Get out the cajon mic and short mic stand. Connect an audio line to the mic and place it in the **Cajon slot** on the stage box.

## **6\. Final Checks & Troubleshooting**

Before the service begins, run through this checklist:

* **Power & Signal:** Does everything have power? Is everything turned on? Does everything have sound?  
* **Board Connections:** Is the blue line plugged into the stage box and in slot **A** on the mixer?  
* **Master Volume:** Is the master sound turned up on the mixer? (Far right on the sound board).  
* **Monitor Issues:** If the monitor has no sound, check if it is muted on the mixer (Bus 8), or if the physical volume knob on the actual monitor speaker is just turned down too low. Remember to check if the main output is up\!  
* **Feedback/Squeaking:** Are things squeaking? Try selecting the offending mic's channel and lowering the gain.  
* **Housekeeping:** Make sure all empty boxes, cases, and bins are stored in the hallway or behind the curtain before the service starts.
